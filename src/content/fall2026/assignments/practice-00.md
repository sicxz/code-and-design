# One source change

**Weekly practice · 10 points · Due 2026-09-27 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific**

## Studio brief

Find the source of one visible change. This is the evidence from [week 0](/1/weeks/week-0/), not an additional project. Finish the same experiment begun in studio; it is included in that week's independent-work estimate.

## Build sequence

1. Open the [Peel Up the Pixels lesson](/1/peel-up-the-pixels/), save your own starter, and identify a heading.
2. Change its words. Then choose one spacing or type-size declaration.
3. Predict that second edit before making it, save, and compare the result.
4. Restore or revise the value until the change is intentional.

## Source check

Name the element, selector/property, your predicted effect, and what you observed.

## Studio evidence

Submit in Canvas:

- **One artifact:** Your saved opening-lesson page or local HTML/CSS files.
- **A brief explanation:** two to four sentences addressing the source check above.
- **One question or next step:** something specific enough for a person to respond to.

## Delivery check and rubric

Open the submitted link while signed out. If publishing or an online service is blocked, attach your current HTML/CSS or planning export with a one-sentence access note. Do not submit a private editor link that only you can open.

The [weekly rubric](/1/resources/rubrics/) awards 4 points for the requested experiment, 4 for its source explanation, and 2 for the question/next step and usable delivery. It does not grade the surrounding project's finish. No extra reflection page, repeated transcript, or decoration is required.

**AI allowance:** Student AI is off. Use the demonstration, documentation, and human help.
