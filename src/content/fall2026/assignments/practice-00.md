# Your workspace

**Weekly assignments · 10 points · Due Sunday, September 27, at 11:59 p.m. Pacific**

Your workspace is the private GitHub repository you use all quarter. You get it in the lesson [Get your workspace](/1/weeks/week-0/get-your-workspace/). Then finish the four items below by Sunday. The README in your repository walks you through each one.

Before writing your introduction, follow [Markdown Tutorial](/1/weeks/week-0/markdown-tutorial/) and add the separate assignment's [Markdown practice section](/1/weeks/week-0/markdown-practice-in-your-readme/) to your README. The Markdown Tutorial has its own Canvas submission. If you already wrote the introduction, keep it and check its preview. You can work on README Steps 4 and 5 while Student Developer Pack approval or CodePen setup is pending; access to your private GitHub repository is still needed.

## What to finish by Sunday

1. **Your first edit.** In your README's **My toolkit** list, replace `[Add your profile URL]` with the address of your GitHub profile. README Step 2 shows how.
2. **Your starting mark.** In `week-0/where-i-am-starting.md`, tick one box under each heading. README Step 4 shows how.
3. **Your introduction.** In `week-0/introduction.md`, write a few sentences under each of the four headings. README Step 5 shows how.
4. **A clean README.** Follow the cleanup step in your own README to delete its setup instructions. Keep the new `## Markdown practice` section below the setup block. An older cleanup guide may list nine sections; your practice section is an additional section.

## Submit in Canvas

1. Open your repository on GitHub.
2. Copy the address from your browser's address bar. It begins `github.com/code-and-design-teaching/desn368-f26-workspace-` and ends with your GitHub username.
3. After the four items above are ready, paste it into [WORKSHOP: Your workspace in Canvas](https://canvas.ewu.edu/courses/1925415/assignments/11774540) and submit. Check Canvas for its submission status and due date.

Only you and the instructor can open your repository.

**AI allowance:** Student AI is off. Use the lessons, the documentation, and human help.
