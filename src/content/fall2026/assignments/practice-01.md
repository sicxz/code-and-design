# Recipe structure check

**Weekly practice · 10 points · Due 2026-10-04 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific**

## Studio brief

Explain one meaningful HTML choice in your recipe. This is the evidence from [week 1](/1/weeks/week-1/), not an additional project. Finish the same experiment begun in studio; it is included in that week's independent-work estimate.

## Build sequence

1. Build the recipe heading, short introduction, ingredient list, and ordered method.
2. Choose one structural decision: list type, nesting, heading sequence, or image/caption.
3. In a copy, make a small change that tests that decision, such as moving a step outside the list.
4. Inspect the result and restore valid, meaningful structure.

## Source check

Explain why the chosen element fits the content and what your experiment revealed.

## Studio evidence

Submit in Canvas:

- **One artifact:** Your current recipe Pen or local files; the project may still be unstyled.
- **A brief explanation:** two to four sentences addressing the source check above.
- **One question or next step:** something specific enough for a person to respond to.

## Delivery check and rubric

Open the submitted link while signed out. If publishing or an online service is blocked, attach your current HTML/CSS or planning export with a one-sentence access note. Do not submit a private editor link that only you can open.

The [weekly rubric](/1/resources/rubrics/) awards 4 points for the requested experiment, 4 for its source explanation, and 2 for the question/next step and usable delivery. It does not grade the surrounding project's finish. No extra reflection page, repeated transcript, or decoration is required.

**AI allowance:** Student AI is off. Human critique and reference examples are welcome.
