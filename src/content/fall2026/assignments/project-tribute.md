# Tribute · A meaningful composition

**Project 2 · 100 points · Due October 25, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific**

## Studio brief

Create one page introducing a person, collective, place, or creative practice that matters to you. Give a reader enough context to understand the subject and a small set of contributions or moments. Build on the recipe's source fluency while introducing a planned composition and flexible layout.

## Build sequence

Use the [tribute starter ZIP](/fall-2026/starters/tribute.zip). Add its files under `tribute/` in the verified student-owned course repository established during the opening handoff; keep the approved course publishing method.

1. Gather two credible source links and write a concise introduction in your own words. Choose three relevant moments or contributions and one permitted image or original illustration. Credit quotations and assets; do not copy a long biography.
2. During October 14 studio, make one Figma wide frame using real content and a small style tile for type, color, and spacing. Make a narrow sketch showing reading order. A paper narrow sketch is acceptable. No separate mood board or multi-screen prototype is required.
3. Build a semantic single-page document: header/name, introduction, figure and caption as appropriate, highlights, source links, footer/home link. Use IDs for actual link destinations and classes for reusable styling; do not add arbitrary IDs to satisfy a list.
4. Use Flexbox for one meaningful relationship, such as an image beside introductory text or a wrapping highlights group. Keep the HTML reading order sensible and test its narrow stack.
5. Use the October 21 critique to identify and repair one real layout problem. Add a title, one-sentence description, and working tribute link to your existing portfolio home.
6. Test the public URL, headings, image alternative text, keyboard focus, readable contrast, and narrow/wide behavior. Commit the final version.

## Source check

Identify the flex container and its direct children. Predict a change to `gap`, wrapping, or a child basis, then make and explain it. Show one cascade or path repair if the instructor requests it. Keep the explanation tied to actual source, not just Figma measurements.

**AI allowance:** after the foundation check, optional explanation or focused debugging help is allowed. Make your own first attempt; do not generate or rewrite the page. Human feedback and documentation remain enough. Add a short disclosure when help was used.

## Studio evidence

Submit the live tribute URL and repository link in Canvas. Link the Figma frame or attach its export and narrow sketch. In four to six sentences, explain one design-to-code decision, one critique repair, and any source/AI assistance. These are project evidence within this one assignment; do not submit a separate design-phase project or reflection page.
