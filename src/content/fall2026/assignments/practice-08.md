# Landing page direction

**Weekly assignments · 10 points · Due 2026-11-22 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific**

## Studio brief

Translate a focused design plan into a source plan. This is the evidence from [week 8](/1/weeks/week-8/), not an additional project. Finish the same experiment begun in studio; it is included in that week's independent-work estimate.

## Build sequence

1. Define the audience, offering, and main action in three short sentences.
2. Plan the real content in a 375px and 1280px frame.
3. Identify one repeated group and the HTML elements/layout tool you intend to use.
4. Get a brief peer reading and make one revision.

## Source check

Explain how one arrangement will change between widths while using the same HTML content. Add the one peer revision.

## Studio evidence

Submit in Canvas:

- **One artifact:** One viewable Figma link or exported image containing the landing page’s narrow/wide frames and compact style tile.
- **A brief explanation:** two to four sentences addressing the source check above.
- **One question or next step:** something specific enough for a person to respond to.

## Delivery check and rubric

Open the submitted link while signed out. If publishing or an online service is blocked, attach your current HTML/CSS or planning export with a one-sentence access note. Do not submit a private editor link that only you can open.

The [weekly rubric](/1/resources/rubrics/) awards 4 points for the requested experiment, 4 for its source explanation, and 2 for the question/next step and usable delivery. It does not grade the surrounding project's finish. No extra reflection page, repeated transcript, or decoration is required.

**AI allowance:** Scoped assistance is optional after source checks. Write your own audience/content claims and explain the plan yourself.
