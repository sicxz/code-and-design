# Publish one manual change

**Weekly practice · 10 points · Due 2026-10-18 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific**

## Studio brief

Trace one manual edit all the way to a published page. This is the evidence from [week 3](/1/weeks/week-3/), not an additional project. Finish the same experiment begun in studio; it is included in that week's independent-work estimate.

## Build sequence

1. Follow the [workspace guide](/1/resources/workflow/) to migrate the recipe manually.
2. Add a working home → recipe → home path.
3. Make one small content edit, commit it, push it, and wait for the Pages deployment to finish.
4. Open the public page separately and verify the changed text.

## Source check

Distinguish saving, committing, pushing, and publishing using your actual edit. Include the relative link you tested.

## Studio evidence

Submit in Canvas:

- **One artifact:** The live portfolio/recipe URL and the GitHub commit URL for one change.
- **A brief explanation:** two to four sentences addressing the source check above.
- **One question or next step:** something specific enough for a person to respond to.

## Delivery check and rubric

Open the submitted link while signed out. If publishing or an online service is blocked, attach your current HTML/CSS or planning export with a one-sentence access note. Do not submit a private editor link that only you can open.

The [weekly rubric](/1/resources/rubrics/) awards 4 points for the requested experiment, 4 for its source explanation, and 2 for the question/next step and usable delivery. It does not grade the surrounding project's finish. No extra reflection page, repeated transcript, or decoration is required.

**AI allowance:** Do the first migration yourself. After the foundation check, optional AI may explain an error; it may not perform the migration or generate your page.
