# Final delivery check

**Weekly practice · 10 points · Due 2026-12-02 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific**

## Studio brief

Verify that a delivery works outside your editing environment. This is the evidence from [week 10](/1/weeks/week-10/), not an additional project. Finish the same experiment begun in studio; it is included in that week's independent-work estimate.

## Build sequence

1. Open the live URL while signed out.
2. Test the main action, one project/return link, and one image.
3. Check 375px, a wide width, keyboard focus, and 200% zoom.
4. Repair the highest-impact failure and verify the saved/pushed version online.

## Source check

Name one specific result and the source responsible for a repair, or explain why a selected behavior works. Include a final next step for the portfolio.

## Studio evidence

Submit in Canvas:

- **One artifact:** Your live landing page or portfolio URL, with a short check record.
- **A brief explanation:** two to four sentences addressing the source check above.
- **One question or next step:** something specific enough for a person to respond to.

## Delivery check and rubric

Open the submitted link while signed out. If publishing or an online service is blocked, attach your current HTML/CSS or planning export with a one-sentence access note. Do not submit a private editor link that only you can open.

The [weekly rubric](/1/resources/rubrics/) awards 4 points for the requested experiment, 4 for its source explanation, and 2 for the question/next step and usable delivery. It does not grade the surrounding project's finish. No extra reflection page, repeated transcript, or decoration is required.

**AI allowance:** Optional scoped help after the source checks. The verification and explanation are yours; this weekly check does not re-grade the landing project.
