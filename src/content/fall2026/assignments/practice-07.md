# One bug, one verified repair

**Weekly assignments · 10 points · Due 2026-11-15 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific**

## Studio brief

Show a complete debugging loop. This is the evidence from [week 7](/1/weeks/week-7/), not an additional project. Finish the same experiment begun in studio.

## Build sequence

1. Reproduce a broken link, missing asset, invalid markup, or layout fault. If necessary, introduce a known fault in a separate copy.
2. Locate its cause with source or browser evidence.
3. State a prediction, make one repair, and test it.
4. Repeat the original test and one neighboring condition. Restore the correct version before publishing.

## Source check

Describe the symptom, exact cause, edit, and verification. A generic statement such as “I fixed CSS” is not enough.

## Studio evidence

Submit in Canvas:

- **One artifact:** Your guide plus one brief before/after description of a verified repair.
- **A brief explanation:** two to four sentences addressing the source check above.
- **One question or next step:** something specific enough for a person to respond to.

## Delivery check and rubric

Open the submitted link while signed out. If publishing or an online service is blocked, attach your current HTML/CSS or planning export with a one-sentence access note. Do not submit a private editor link that only you can open.

The [weekly rubric](/1/resources/rubrics/) awards 4 points for the requested experiment, 4 for its source explanation, and 2 for the question/next step and usable delivery. It does not grade the surrounding project's finish. No extra reflection page, repeated transcript, or decoration is required.

**AI allowance:** Optional scoped assistance after the source checks; perform and explain your repair yourself. Do not invent an AI failure.
