# Responsive evidence

**Weekly assignments · 10 points · Due 2026-11-08 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific**

## Studio brief

Explain a responsive decision using evidence from two widths. This is the evidence from [week 6](/1/weeks/week-6/), not an additional project. Finish the same experiment begun in studio; it is included in that week's independent-work estimate.

## Build sequence

1. Resize gradually and identify where your guide becomes crowded.
2. Choose or revise a breakpoint based on that observation.
3. Test just below and above it, plus a narrow width.
4. Confirm that navigation and reading order still make sense.

## Source check

Name the breakpoint, the rule it changes, and the content need that motivated it. Include the widths you actually tested.

## Studio evidence

Submit in Canvas:

- **One artifact:** Your field-guide URL and one combined image or two screenshots showing the relevant narrow/wide behavior.
- **A brief explanation:** two to four sentences addressing the source check above.
- **One question or next step:** something specific enough for a person to respond to.

## Delivery check and rubric

Open the submitted link while signed out. If publishing or an online service is blocked, attach your current HTML/CSS or planning export with a one-sentence access note. Do not submit a private editor link that only you can open.

The [weekly rubric](/1/resources/rubrics/) awards 4 points for the requested experiment, 4 for its source explanation, and 2 for the question/next step and usable delivery. It does not grade the surrounding project's finish. No extra reflection page, repeated transcript, or decoration is required.

**AI allowance:** Optional scoped help only after the applicable source checks. Your breakpoint decision and explanation must be your own.
