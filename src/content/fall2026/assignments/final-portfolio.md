# Portfolio and source presentation

**Final portfolio · 100 points · Due December 9, 2026 at noon Pacific**  
**Presentations: December 9, 1–3 p.m.**

## Studio brief

Show what you have built and what you understand. Curate the recipe, tribute, field guide, and landing page inside the same repository and portfolio home you have maintained since week 3. The final is a clear collection and explanation, not a fifth large project.

## Build sequence

1. Keep the root `index.html` and the existing `recipe/`, `tribute/`, `field-guide/`, and `landing/` folders. Repair links in place; do not reorganize the whole repository during finals.
2. Write a short introduction that tells a reader who you are as a developing designer. Include four project entries, each with a title, one or two sentences about its purpose/learning, and a working link. A personal photograph, social accounts, and custom thumbnail system are optional.
3. Check the portfolio's own type hierarchy, spacing, narrow/wide layout, keyboard focus, and 200% zoom. Link to your repository. Each project should provide a route back home.
4. Keep asset/tutorial/AI source notes concise and accessible. You can link existing project notes; do not copy every credit into several places. Update the README with the live portfolio link and a compact project/source index.
5. Write one short paragraph naming a specific change in your understanding and a concrete next learning step. Reuse that thinking in the presentation; a separate reflection page and five essays are not required.
6. Open the published home while signed out and click through all four projects and back. Check the latest pushed version, then submit by noon on December 9.

## Source presentation

Prepare approximately three minutes:

- **30 seconds:** introduce the collection and one project you want the group to inspect.
- **60 seconds:** connect a visual decision in that project to its actual HTML/CSS.
- **60 seconds:** make a small fresh change chosen with the instructor or a peer, then explain the observed result. Close AI tools during this independent demonstration.
- **30 seconds:** name one concrete improvement in your understanding and your next step.

Bring a local copy of the source and a view of the page as a connectivity backup. If you need an alternative presentation format, arrange it with the instructor in advance. A slide deck is not required.

## Studio evidence

Submit the live portfolio URL and repository URL. Add the short growth paragraph if it is not already on the portfolio or README. Mention any specific known limitation rather than hiding it. Project source links remain evidence of earlier work; the final does not re-grade each project.

## Delivery check and rubric

The [portfolio rubric](/1/resources/rubrics/) awards curation/introduction 30 points, navigation/delivery 25, portfolio readability/access 20, and source presentation/growth 25. Use the common performance levels. The existing four project grades remain in the 40% projects category; this assignment belongs only in the 10% portfolio category.

Optional scoped AI help follows the existing policy for small repairs, with disclosure. The growth paragraph and live source explanation must express your own understanding. Avoid a new visual direction that consumes time needed to repair actual links, reading problems, or source gaps.
