# Explain a layout repair

**Weekly practice · 10 points · Due 2026-10-25 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific**

## Studio brief

Use evidence to repair one layout problem. This is the evidence from [week 4](/1/weeks/week-4/), not an additional project. Finish the same experiment begun in studio; it is included in that week's independent-work estimate.

## Build sequence

1. Test the tribute at a narrow width and choose one observable issue.
2. Inspect the responsible flex container, child, or competing rule.
3. Predict a specific edit, make it, and test the original condition.
4. Check a second width to see whether the repair holds.

## Source check

Name the cause and the rule you changed. Explain what now works and how you verified it.

## Studio evidence

Submit in Canvas:

- **One artifact:** Your tribute page and a short before/after description; one comparison image is optional.
- **A brief explanation:** two to four sentences addressing the source check above.
- **One question or next step:** something specific enough for a person to respond to.

## Delivery check and rubric

Open the submitted link while signed out. If publishing or an online service is blocked, attach your current HTML/CSS or planning export with a one-sentence access note. Do not submit a private editor link that only you can open.

The [weekly rubric](/1/resources/rubrics/) awards 4 points for the requested experiment, 4 for its source explanation, and 2 for the question/next step and usable delivery. It does not grade the surrounding project's finish. No extra reflection page, repeated transcript, or decoration is required.

**AI allowance:** Optional explanations/debugging only after the foundation check. Make your initial prediction yourself; disclose any help in the existing notes.
