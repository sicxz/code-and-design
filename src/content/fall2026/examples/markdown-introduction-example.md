# Sample introduction (an example, not a real student)

## What do you think a designer is?
Someone who chooses how a page looks and works for its readers.

## What do you think a coder is?
Someone who writes the instructions that make the page work.

## What do you want to make?
I want to make a page about the plants on my windowsill.

## What do you think intelligence is?
I am not sure yet. I want to ask what counts as intelligence and why.
