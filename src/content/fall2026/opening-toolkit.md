## Week 0: make one saved CodePen

1. Create or sign into [CodePen](https://codepen.io/). Choose a professional username you would be comfortable sharing with future employers.
2. Open the [Peel Up the Pixels starter](/1/peel-up-the-pixels/) and use the [workspace guide](/1/resources/workflow/) for the HTML and CSS panels.
3. Give your Pen a meaningful title, save your version, and copy its URL. Make a small edit, save again, close the editor tab, and reopen the saved link.
4. Check that someone else can view it. Submit that link and the evidence requested in the assignment. Keep a local backup; if the service is blocked, submit the starter's HTML and CSS files in Canvas.

<h2 id="opening-course-accounts-workspace-and-figma">Opening half-week: course accounts, workspace, and Figma</h2>

Begin the required account and workspace setup during the opening half-week and weekend. Account approval and permission to use AI are separate; creating an account does not change the independent AI-free foundation period.

1. Create or update your [GitHub account](https://github.com/join) using your `@ewu.edu` email. Verify the email and choose a professional username. Add your name and a short bio, such as “DESN 368 student learning web design,” to your profile. A photo, location, and website are optional.
2. Create or sign into [CodePen](https://codepen.io/) and [Figma](https://www.figma.com/). Begin the GitHub Education application through the current course instructions; approval may arrive later.
3. Learn the repository vocabulary and operations used in the course before changing files independently.
4. Follow the instructor's verified clone lesson once the source repository and student-owned destination have been approved. Do not fork the instructor repository.
5. Save, reopen, and push one small change through the verified route before relying on the workspace for course work.

The exact clone, destination, edit, save, push, and reopen steps remain withheld from this draft until they have been rehearsed end to end. Canvas and the instructor will identify the assigned step once the procedure is approved.

## Weekend reading and handwritten visual notes

Read [“Code is Clay”](https://campedersen.com/code-is-clay) by Cam Pedersen and [“Making space for a handmade web”](https://www.figma.com/blog/making-space-for-a-handmade-web/) by Chia Amisola. Use pen, words, and sketches to connect the readings to what you want to make and to your first source experiment.
