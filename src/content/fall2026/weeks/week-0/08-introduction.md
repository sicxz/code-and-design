Two files in your `week-0` folder hold this lesson's work. Steps 4 and 5 of your README walk you through both.

## Mark where you are starting

Open `week-0/where-i-am-starting.md` in your repository. Tick one box under each heading. Then write one line about what you hope to make by the end of the quarter. No one grades this file. It does not measure your ability. You answer it again at the end of the quarter.

## Write your introduction

Open `week-0/introduction.md`. Write a few sentences under each of its four headings:

- What do you think a designer is?
- What do you think a coder is?
- What do you want to make?
- What do you think intelligence is?

Plain words are fine. Commit each file when you finish it.

On Monday, September 28, the same words become HTML headings and paragraphs. You will not add an explanation or reflection.
