Now work through one of the learning paths allowed by [APPLY: Markdown Tutorial in Canvas](https://canvas.ewu.edu/courses/1925415/assignments/11775193). Do this before you write the **Markdown practice** section in your own README.

## Choose one path

- Work through all seven lessons at [Markdown Tutorial](https://www.markdowntutorial.com/), **or**
- Read [GitHub's “Getting started with Markdown” article](https://github.blog/developer-skills/github/github-for-beginners-getting-started-with-markdown/).

These are the two choices in the Canvas assignment. Its **Due** field is the place to check your deadline.

## Bring the practice back

Your Markdown proof lives in your private GitHub repository; you submit its link in Canvas. In the next lesson, you will add `## Markdown practice` at the end of `README.md` and show the features Canvas names: emphasis, headings, two kinds of links, an image with alternative text, a blockquote, two kinds of lists, and paragraphs with a hard line break. The next lesson includes the reference-link and line-break steps if you chose the GitHub article.
