# Week 10 · Finish, test, and curate

**Monday, November 30 and Wednesday, December 2 · 1–3:30 p.m.**  
Independent work estimate: **7 hours total**, including the landing page and portfolio preparation. Final presentations: **December 9, 1–3 p.m.** Portfolio delivery: **December 9 at noon**.

## Studio brief

Finish the landing page by December 2. Then use the work already in your repository to make a coherent portfolio. This is a repair and curation period, not a full redesign or an opportunity to add another large project.

## Build sequence

**Monday, November 30: landing-page test clinic.**

- **1:00–1:15:** write a short repair list and rank it: broken behavior first, unreadable content next, visual refinement last.
- **1:15–1:40:** instructor demonstrates testing a published page at narrow/intermediate/wide widths and finding the relevant source for one failure.
- **1:40–2:10:** partner test: main action, reading order, links, images, keyboard focus, and 200% zoom. Record observable results.
- **2:10–2:20:** break.
- **2:20–3:05:** repair the highest-impact issues. Remove incomplete optional features if they prevent a clear, functional core.
- **3:05–3:25:** explain one repair independently and retest the original condition.
- **3:25–3:30:** commit a delivery candidate.

**Wednesday, December 2: deliver and curate.**

- **1:00–1:15:** open the submitted candidate from its public URL; confirm it matches the latest commit.
- **1:15–1:40:** short landing-page show-and-tell. Each person names the audience, main action, and one source decision.
- **1:40–2:10:** finish core landing repairs, credits, and the project brief's delivery requirements.
- **2:10–2:20:** break.
- **2:20–3:05:** open the existing portfolio home. Add or improve its four project entries, introduction, source/repository link, and return paths.
- **3:05–3:25:** rehearse a source explanation with a partner. Select code you understand and can modify, not merely the most complex feature.
- **3:25–3:30:** submit the [final delivery check](/1/assignments/practice-10/) and identify the few remaining portfolio repairs.

The [landing project](/1/assignments/project-landing/) is due December 2 at 11:59 p.m. Weekly evidence evaluates one delivery check; it does not re-grade the landing page.

## Portfolio preparation · December 3–8

Read the [portfolio brief](/1/assignments/final-portfolio/). Keep the same root `index.html`; do not rename every folder or migrate hosting. Confirm that recipe, tribute, field guide, and landing page each have a title, a short useful introduction, and a working link. Repair the portfolio's own layout and focus states. Add a concise source/AI disclosure where needed.

Prepare a three-minute presentation: about 30 seconds to introduce the work, 60 seconds to connect a design decision to source, 60 seconds for an instructor/peer-selected change and explanation, and 30 seconds for a concrete next learning step. Keep a local copy or PDF/screenshot plus source files as a connectivity backup. Do not make a new slide deck unless it helps an agreed presentation format.

## Source check

Trace a project card link from the portfolio root into its folder and back. Then make a fresh small edit in the chosen project and explain its result. Your final explanation samples your understanding; rehearsing a script is not a substitute for understanding the actual source.

## Studio evidence and delivery

The portfolio is due **December 9 at noon Pacific**. Final presentations run **1–3 p.m.** in the course's final session. Open the public portfolio while signed out, test all four projects and source links, and check the version again after any final push. Portfolio scoring evaluates curation and explanation; it does not repeat the project grades. Bring any remaining issue with its source and a concrete plan for repair.
