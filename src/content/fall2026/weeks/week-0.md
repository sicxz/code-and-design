## Continue with Week 0

If you made the small profile-URL edit during Wednesday's studio, keep it. Open your private repository and check that the change is there. If you missed the class setup, use [Get your workspace](/1/weeks/week-0/get-your-workspace/) to catch up. You can return to [Markdown: source and preview](/1/weeks/week-0/markdown/) and [Practice Markdown live](/1/weeks/week-0/practice-markdown/) whenever you need them; that site's practice editor does not save your work.

From there, follow this path:

1. Do [Markdown Tutorial](/1/weeks/week-0/markdown-tutorial/), then add [Markdown practice in your README](/1/weeks/week-0/markdown-practice-in-your-readme/). Submit that repository link to its Canvas assignment as soon as the proof is ready.
2. Complete [your starting mark and introduction](/1/weeks/week-0/introduction/) in your repository. You may do these while Student Developer Pack approval or other account setup is still pending.
3. Make [visual notes on the two readings](/1/weeks/week-0/readings-and-visual-notes/) and [your Figma card](/1/weeks/week-0/your-figma-card/). These two branches can be done in either order. Submit each in Canvas when ready.
4. Try the [Week 0 check](/1/weeks/week-0/week-0-quiz/), then [confirm your four submissions](/1/weeks/week-0/submit-and-return/) in Canvas.

The [Learning Passport](/1/passport/) is an optional way to move your site place markers between browsers. A green check or percentage on this site records your place here; it does not confirm a Canvas submission.
